**THE OBJECT**
A large, exceptionally detailed Duramax V8 engine positioned in the center of a clean, realistic engineering workshop.
The engine must have:
- Exactly 8 cylinders arranged in two banks of 4.
- Visible pistons, connecting rods, crankshaft, camshafts and valves.
- Detailed cylinder heads, intake manifold, exhaust headers, bolts, pipes and mechanical components.
- Realistic metallic materials, machining details, rubber, steel and aluminum.
- Accurate proportions and convincing mechanical construction.
**REAL MECHANICAL ANIMATION**
The engine must actually function as a synchronized mechanical simulation.
- All 8 pistons reciprocate with realistic crankshaft-driven motion.
- Connecting rods remain mechanically attached to the pistons and crankshaft.
- Crankshaft rotates continuously while the engine runs.
- Camshafts rotate at half the crankshaft speed.
- Intake and exhaust valves open and close in a plausible four-stroke sequence.
- Adjust RPM from 0 to 4,000 using an interactive slider.
- Smoothly animate changes in engine speed.
- Provide a slow-motion mode to inspect mechanical movement.
**3D EXPLORATION**
- WASD + mouse for first-person walking.
- Mouse orbit mode for inspecting the engine.
- Smooth zoom and camera movement.
- Realistic perspective and scale.
- Allow walking around the entire engine and inspecting it closely.
**INTERACTIVE FEATURES**
START / STOP — start and stop engine operation.
RPM CONTROL — control engine speed.
CUTAWAY MODE — reveal the moving internal mechanism by making exterior components transparent.
EXPLODED VIEW — smoothly separate major engine components to show their construction.
ASSEMBLE — restore every component to its original position.
PART INSPECTION — click any major component to highlight it and display its name.
RESET CAMERA — restore the default viewing angle.
