# 6.6L Duramax LLY (RPO LLY, 2004.5–2005) — model brief

> Development research notes, kept separate from the shipped interface copy. The visualisation includes a live calibration toggle supporting both the **Allison 1000 Automatic (`605 lb-ft / 310 hp / 26,000 psi`)** and **ZF S6-650 6-Speed Manual (`520 lb-ft / 300 hp / 23,000 psi`)** ratings, as well as real-time Web Audio API synthesis (`src/audio.js`); see the accuracy notes in [`README.md`](README.md).

**Orientation.** 90° V8, longitudinal; #1 cylinder at the passenger-side front (`-X` when facing forward `+Z`, odds `1-3-5-7` passenger, evens `2-4-6-8` driver) with clockwise front crankshaft rotation. Front = gear/accessory drive; rear = flywheel.

**External layout.** CP3: front of engine, low in the valley between the heads, flange-mounted to the block/front cover and gear-driven (not timed); buried under the Y-bridge, only its top and fittings visible. Outlet feeds the passenger-side (RH) rail; a rear crossover feeds the driver-side (LH) rail; the FPRV is at the back of the LH rail. Turbo: GT3788VA in the valley at the REAR — turbine housing at the back, compressor forward, inlet mouthpiece pointing forward/passenger, VGT solenoid and vane-position sensor on the unit, oil feed/drain and coolant lines attached, downpipe dropping behind the engine on the passenger side. Two up-pipes run from the outboard exhaust manifolds (driver and passenger logs, heat-shielded) to the turbine inlet. Intake: cast-aluminum Y-bridge on top/front, valley intake manifold; cold-side intercooler pipe passenger side, hot-side driver side. EGR: valve flanged to the rear of the intake manifold; long rectangular stainless cooler on the passenger side of the rear valley, fed by a Y in the passenger up-pipe, with two coolant hoses. Oil filter: driver side, low, aft of the front wheel under the floor/frame rail, horizontal spin-on (PF2232). Oil pan: deep cast-aluminum sump, black. Front: fan clutch/fan center, crank damper, big cast-aluminum gear cover; A/C compressor low passenger side; alternator passenger side high (uncertain — some photos suggest driver side); P/S pump driver side. Valve covers: cast aluminum, one per bank; LLY injectors and rails sit outside the covers, exposed. A black plastic acoustic cover with DURAMAX branding hides the valley.

**Valvetrain.** 32 valves (4/cyl), 16 pushrods, 16 shaft-mounted forged rockers, 16 mechanical roller lifters, 16 cam lobes on one cam-in-block (`y = 150 mm`, driven 0.5× by 2:1 helical timing gears). Each rocker drives a guideless bridge that opens two valves — 16 bridges; two intake + two exhaust per cylinder around a central vertical injector. Valves sit in a flat fire deck, near-vertical (≈0–5° splay), 45° seats; exact OEM inclination not verified. Valve lift ≈0.40 in (10 mm), estimated from ~0.27 in lobe × ~1.5 rocker — not an OEM published figure. Head deck: flat, 6 × 14 mm bolts/cyl (shared), 0.037 × 4.115 in gasket, 0.012 in piston protrusion, 1.358 in compression height, 0.500 in pin; derived crank-center-to-deck ≈9.71 in.

**Numbers.** Firing order 1-2-7-8-4-5-6-3. Bore 4.055 in (103 mm), stroke 3.898 in (99 mm), 403 ci; rod 6.417 in; throw 1.949 in; CR 17.5:1. Bosch CP3 common rail, 23,200 psi (1,600 bar), solenoid injectors. Turbo: 62.8 mm compressor inducer / 88 mm exducer, 11 blades; turbine 71.3 / 65.7 mm, 13 blades; vane height 14.4 mm; stock boost ≈23 psi (30 psi is a tuned figure). 310 hp @ 3,000, 520 lb-ft @ 1,600 (2004.5–05); some 2006 LLY references list 605 lb-ft. Idle ≈650–700 rpm; governed 3,200–3,250 rpm. Oil 10 qt with filter; dry mass 835 lb; ≈30 × 30 × 32 in.

**Visual cues.** Black block/pan and cover; bare aluminum heads, valve covers, intake/Y-bridge; rusty cast-iron exhaust manifolds and turbine housing; aluminum compressor housing; zinc/greenish steel hard lines; black injector connectors; silver heat shields. Turbo is compact and half-hidden; VGT solenoid is a black cylinder with a 2-wire connector. EGR cooler is a long rectangular stainless box with two hose necks. CP3 is a small ribbed aluminum pump with 3 plungers and banjo fittings. Passenger side shows downpipe, EGR, turbo inlet, A/C; driver side shows P/S pump, oil filter, shielded up-pipe. Casting marks: “GM” and part numbers; “DURAMAX” on the cover.

**Claim check.** “Piezoelectric injectors” — false for LLY; Bosch solenoid, external. Piezo arrives on the LML (2011–16). “Valley/rear-mounted turbo” — true. “Valve bridge architecture” — true (16 bridges). 32-valve OHV and firing order — true. “2004–2006” is loose: LLY is 2004.5–2005 (some early 2006); 2006 is mostly LBZ (360 hp/650 lb-ft). The 605 lb-ft “2006 LLY” figure is contested.
